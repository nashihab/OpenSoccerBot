FlySky FS-i6 / FS-i6X + FS-iA6B is one reference setup. The firmware uses standard PWM receiver outputs.

04 / POWER

Battery builder

Model the pack before buying cells. The TB6612-based builds documented here stay at 2S or 3S.

POWER PATH

Protection first

Battery +→Fuse→Master switch→Motor rail
↳ Buck / BEC → controller + receiver + servo
  • Keep motor current off the MCU supply rail.
  • Place the fuse close to battery positive.
  • Size wire, connector and driver for measured current.
